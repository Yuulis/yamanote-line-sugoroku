Reading of the findings (not legal advice):

- **No registered mark for 「山手線」 alone was found, and none owned by JR East
  covers games (9/28/41-game services).** JR East's only 山手線-bearing mark
  found is 「東京まるっと山手線」 for transport and events. A 2015 Q&A stating that
  railway line names are generally not registered matches this
  (https://detail.chiebukuro.yahoo.co.jp/qa/question_detail/q14143137489 — low
  reliability source, consistent with the mirror data).
- **Closest registered right: 望月商店 「YAMANOTE山手YAMANOTE」 (class 28 includes
  すごろく; class 9 includes home video game programs).** Its reading is
  ヤマノテ; ours is ヤマノテセンスゴロク, where 山手線 is a well-known proper name
  of a railway line with a distinct meaning. A browser game offered free is
  closer to class 41 (online game provision) or downloadable programs than to
  the listed goods. Risk looks low, but this is the record a professional
  would look at first.
- JR East has ~600 trademark filings (rough count reported by a blogger,
  https://manager-travels.com/35939729/) and registers names in many goods
  classes (e.g. 登録5755771, classes 9/14/16/18:
  https://tokkyo-lab.com/chizai/midikanachizai-train). Registrations older than
  the mirror's ~20-year window were not covered.
- Non-trademark risk: even without a registered mark, presenting the game so it
  looks official could raise 不正競争防止法 (misleading indication of source)
  concerns. D4 (2026-10-16) removed the 「非公式のファンメイド作品」 sentence; that
  was an informed user choice, recorded here only because it is relevant to
  this risk.

## 3. Logo mark ("loop ring + die")

No existing mark combining a closed loop with a die was found. The nearby
designs to stay clear of:

|-----------------|-------------------|--------|
| JR East station numbering (2016-) | Line code 「JY」 + 2-digit number inside a line-colour frame (rounded square), black three-letter code tab above major stations | https://www.itmedia.co.jp/business/articles/1604/06/news106.html , https://www.tetsudo.com/news/1539/ |
| Yamanote line colour | ウグイス色 / 黄緑6号; JR East web uses #85C023 (also Yokohama Line) | https://dic.nicovideo.jp/a/%E9%89%84%E9%81%93%E8%B7%AF%E7%B7%9A%E3%81%AE%E3%83%A9%E3%82%A4%E3%83%B3%E3%82%AB%E3%83%A9%E3%83%BC%E4%B8%80%E8%A6%A7 |

| JR / JR East corporate logo | JR letterforms | — |
| 桃太郎電鉄 | Title logo is Kanteiryū lettering; no ring/die emblem found | https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%A1%83%E5%A4%AA%E9%83%8E%E9%9B%BB%E9%89%84%E3%82%B7%E3%83%AA%E3%83%BC%E3%82%BA |

Design cautions so the original mark stays clearly distinct:

1. Do not make the ring a solid line-colour circle with letters or digits
   inside (reads as Tokyo Metro/Toei numbering); do not use a rounded-square
   frame with two capital letters (reads as JR 「JY」).
2. Do not make Yamanote green (#85C023, #9ACD32-like 黄緑) the dominant ring
   colour, and do not pair green with a black three-letter tab.
3. Do not build the ring from **30 segments, 30 dots or 30 ticks**, even
   though the game has 30 segments — JR East's 100th-anniversary logo uses
   exactly "30 parts". Use a continuous or clearly differently-divided loop.
4. Let the die carry the identity (pips, perspective, rounded die body)
   and keep the ring abstract (not a track with sleepers, not a train
   silhouette, not the E235 front).
5. No JR letterforms, no station-name-sign (駅名標) layout, no gold ring around
   a train.
6. Once drawn, run a J-PlatPat figure search (steps below) on the final
   artwork before publishing; the mark could not be checked as an image here.

## 4. Recommendation

- **Name 「山手線すごろく」: GO with cautions.**
  - Risk of conflict with a registered mark looks low (no ヤマノテセン/ヤマノテセンスゴロク
    registration in game classes; JR East's only 山手線 mark is for transport
    and events).
  - Cautions: (a) the name is descriptive and already used by others, so it is
    not exclusive and would be hard to register yourself; (b) confirm the
    望月商店 「YAMANOTE山手YAMANOTE」 record and the unreviewed ヤマノテ records
    in J-PlatPat; (c) avoid JR East's own wording such as 「まるっと山手線」;
    (d) consider a short non-affiliation line (e.g. 「JR東日本ほか各鉄道事業者とは関係ありません」)
    in the about/credits area — this revisits D4 and is the user's call.
- **Subtitle 「東京近郊版」: GO.**
- **Mark: GO with cautions** — follow the six design cautions in §3 and do the
  figure search on the final artwork.
- Fallback names: not required at the current risk level. If the user wants a
  more distinctive, registrable brand, candidates to check the same way
  (none were searched): 「ぐるりすごろく 東京近郊版」, 「一周すごろく 東京近郊版」,
  「環状すごろく 東京近郊版」, 「サイコロ乗り鉄 東京近郊」.

J-PlatPat steps (https://www.j-platpat.inpit.go.jp/):

1. メニュー「商標」→「商標検索」.
2. 検索対象: keep 「国内文献」 and include both 登録 and 出願 (pending)
   documents.
3. 検索項目「称呼（類似検索）」, enter ヤマノテセンスゴロク → 検索. Repeat
   separately with ヤマノテセン, ヤマノテ, スゴロク.
4. For each run, add a condition row 「区分」 = 09, then 28, then 41 (or enter
   all three, space-separated, if the field accepts it) to narrow the list.
5. Open each hit's 経過情報/詳細 and check: 権利者 (watch for 東日本旅客鉄道株式会社,
   株式会社望月商店), 指定商品・指定役務 (ゲーム, おもちゃ, すごろく, プログラム,
   オンラインゲームの提供), and 存続/消滅 status.
6. Owner check: 検索項目「権利者/出願人」 = 東日本旅客鉄道 with 区分 09 / 28 / 41,
   and skim for any 山手線 or ring/die figure marks.
7. Figure check after the logo is drawn: 「図形等分類」 search — look up the
   Vienna codes for dice/games and for circles/rings in the 図形等分類表 linked
   on the search page, combine with 区分 09/28/41, and compare visually.
8. If any hit is in a game class with a similar reading or figure, consult a
   弁理士 before promotion.
